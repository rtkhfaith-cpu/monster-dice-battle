## 01 — GLITCH ALLEY

**Visual:** Cracked asphalt, neon pawn shops, broken ATMs with teeth, vending shrines  
**Atmosphere:** First-night-in-the-city; everything wrong at 2AM  
**Music:** Lo-fi hip-hop + dial-up; plastic percussion  
**Corruption:** UI Bleed — pop-up hazards, fake button traps  
**Normals:** Pop-Up Gremlin, ATM Mimic, Scam Rat, Coupon Slime  
**Mini boss:** Terms & Conditions (lawyer spirit of unread scrolls)  
**Big boss:** The Infinite Sale (discount deity; stats drop “for limited time”)  
**Hazards:** Misclick pits, ad walls, captcha fog  
**Flavour:** Handlers joke this is “tutorial hell.” It isn’t—it’s where the world learned polite manipulation.
