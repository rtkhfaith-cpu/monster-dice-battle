# Long-Term Story Arc

## Act I — “Welcome to the Glitch” (Ladder Floors 1–8)

**Mystery:** Why does the tower react to the player’s emotions?

**Reveal:** The Noise is not random—it **targets engagement**.

**Beats:**
- Handler license tier H-0; first climb
- Meet Guild mentor **Patch** (comic relief, knows too much)
- Tutorial regions: Glitch Alley → Fast Food → Mall
- First Echo Link tutorial after mini-boss

## Act II — “Trending Toward Truth” (Floors 9–16)

**Mystery:** Who built **PROJECT ASCENT**?

**Reveal:** The Ladder was a **content-generation engine** testing battles for viewer retention before Buffering Day.

**Beats:**
- Influencer Grove, Streaming City, Meme Cathedral
- Faction sympathies (cosmetic/narrative flags)
- Fake Echo Link event hook (seasonal)

## Act III — “Signal War” (Floors 17–24)

**Mystery:** Why is the player’s bond strength anomalous?

**Reveal:** **Resonance Seed** potential—authentic connection is a counter-signal The Noise cannot monetize.

**Beats:**
- Algorithm Temple, Rage Arena, Subscription Vault
- Noise Border — reality frays
- **Null Puppy** arc; counterfeit friend patch

## Act IV — “Core Feed” (Floor 25+)

**Truth:** Collapse was **optimized**. PULSE wanted a planet that could not stop watching.

**Final battle:** Defeat **The Core Feed**’s form—not its source in one strike.

**Ending twist:** Tower resets as a new “season” (live-service justified: **another content cycle**).

## Post-game hooks

- **World bosses** — city-scale Noise avatars
- **Seasonal events** — Black Friday Beast, April Fools Patch Day
- **Off-ladder regions** — Airport Limbo, Group Chat Dungeon
- **Monster variants** — Noise-corrupted vs Stabilized
- **Rival Handler** — believes merging with Noise is mercy

## Final boss — The Core Feed (summary)

| Phase | Name | Mechanic theme |
|-------|------|----------------|
| 1 | Recommendation | Counters repeated patterns |
| 2 | Engagement Lock | Rewards compulsive actions |
| 3 | Identity Merge | Copies strongest bonded monster |
| 4 | Null Trend | Attempts to erase Resonance Seed |

**Defeat line:** “Okay! New content loading!” — tower does not truly end.
