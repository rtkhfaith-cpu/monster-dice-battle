# The Noise — Major Factions

Each faction influences monster design, environments, and audio.

---

## 1. Toxic Feed

- **Domains:** Influencer Grove, Rage Arena
- **Monsters:** Poison, charm, social DOT, comparison debuffs
- **Environments:** Ring lights, comment toxicity, beauty hazards
- **Audio:** Sweet melody + venom bass; camera shutter percussion

## 2. Viral Swarm

- **Domains:** Outbreak City, Meme Cathedral (edges)
- **Monsters:** Spread mechanics, summons, exposure scaling
- **Environments:** Quarantine loops, viewership meters
- **Audio:** Crowd panic + drill; breaking-news stabs

## 3. Lag Cult

- **Domains:** Lag Dimension, Broken Internet Realm
- **Monsters:** Delay, desync, phantom turns
- **Environments:** Stutter terrain, rollback tiles
- **Audio:** Half-time EDM; clock tick combat cues

## 4. Burnout Legion

- **Domains:** Burnout Industrial Zone, Subscription Vault
- **Monsters:** Stress scaling, resource tax, meeting freeze
- **Environments:** Fluorescent offices, calendar fortresses
- **Audio:** Printer jam rhythm; sigh choirs

## 5. Fast Food Syndicate

- **Domains:** Fast Food Kingdom, Sewer tributaries
- **Monsters:** Caloric burst, grease armor, combo chains
- **Environments:** Oil geysers, loyalty card binding
- **Audio:** Kitchen trap; jingle horror on bosses

## 6. Glitch Spirits

- **Domains:** Glitch Alley, Glitch Forest, Tech Yard
- **Monsters:** Random resist, texture corruption, UI bleed
- **Environments:** Pop-up traps, compression artifacts
- **Audio:** Error tones as melody; folktronica wrongness

## 7. Bacteria Hive

- **Domains:** Sewer Depths, Bacteria Hive Metro
- **Monsters:** Infection stack, split reproduction
- **Environments:** Warm fog, fever heat, wet floors
- **Audio:** Microbial techno; squish percussion

## 8. Forgotten Apps

- **Domains:** App Graveyard, Abandoned Streaming City
- **Monsters:** Deprecation, memory drain, nostalgia heal
- **Environments:** Uninstall winds, legacy code vines
- **Audio:** Broken chiptune lullabies

## 9. Doomscroll Monks

- **Domains:** Doomscroll Monastery, Emotion Dumpster borders
- **Monsters:** Fatigue scaling, counter, truth debuffs
- **Environments:** Infinite stairs, headline bursts
- **Audio:** Dark ambient + scroll wheel; whisper notifications

---

## Faction relationships

| Faction | Allies (loose) | Opposes |
|---------|----------------|---------|
| Toxic Feed | Viral Swarm | Doomscroll Monks |
| Lag Cult | Glitch Spirits | Fast Food (impatience) |
| Burnout Legion | Forgotten Apps | Influencer cults |
| Fast Food Syndicate | Bacteria Hive | — |
| Doomscroll Monks | Emotion realms | Toxic Feed |

Handlers do not join factions—they **stabilize** monsters pulled from them.
