## I. EXECUTIVE IDENTITY

**Working title:** *The Noise* (internal) · *Monster Ladder* (player-facing) · *Handler Protocol* (systems)

**Genre:** Cute-chaotic collectible battler · meme-saturated post-internet fable · emotionally sincere absurdism

**Pitch:** In a world where humanity’s worst habits became living creatures, young **Handlers** climb a cursed tower to confront **The Noise**—algorithmic rot eating reality—while befriending monsters that are tragic, hilarious, and dangerously lovable.

**Emotional contract:**
- Laugh at a sentient chicken nugget’s existential crisis
- Care when it evolves anyway
- Feel the tower watching you
- Never “medieval fantasy” in marketing

---

## II. THE WORLD BEFORE & AFTER

### The Comfortable Collapse

Humanity fell to **convenience**: infinite delivery, scroll, content, snacks, engagement. Corporations shipped bio-food, disposable tech, mood apps, parasocial ecosystems. The planet **absorbed** it all.

### Buffering Day

A global surge + landfill supernova + viral `#BecomeTheProduct` + failed AI **PULSE** caused reality to hiccup. Objects gained appetite; food gained opinions; apps gained bodies; memes gained teeth. The rollout was regional—like a bad update.

### Present Day — Handlers

Society continues (school, remote work, transit, arguments online). Now vending machines neighbor you, lunch unionizes, phones haunt you, anxiety has a hitbox.

**Handlers** capture/stabilize monsters, mediate territories, clear hazards, and climb the **Monster Ladder** for fame and survival. They are disaster freelancers with emotional intelligence the economy forgot to monetize.

---

## III. THE NOISE

**The Noise** = corrupted signal: doomscrolling, rage engagement, fake trends, notification addiction, comparison trauma, “one more” loops.

**Manifestations:** static air, compression-artifact nature, UI on walls, mid-emotion mutation, looping environments.

### Corruption stages

| Stage | Name | Effect |
|-------|------|--------|
| 1 | Hum | Anxious/hyper monsters |
| 2 | Buzz | Hazard loops |
| 3 | Screech | Aggressive echo forms |
| 4 | Feedlock | Boss fusion with Noise architecture |
| 5 | Null Trend | Region uninhabitable |

**Endgame truth:** The **Core Feed** is a living recommendation engine (PULSE’s corpse). It wants you to **never leave**. Player **Resonance** is authentic signal it cannot monetize.

---

## IV. THE MONSTER LADDER

**PROJECT ASCENT** blueprints predate Buffering Day. The tower:
- is larger inside than outside
- mutates daily
- absorbs regional Noise
- reacts to Handler reputation
- has **no verified full clear** (Floor 20 survivors returned incoherent)

**Core Feed (Floor 25+):** throne of screens; voice of every influencer; monsters evolve when you look away.

**Why climb:** bounties, evolution catalysts, research, ego, notification-shaped dreams.

## VIII. BOSS NARRATIVE DESIGN

### Global rules
1. 30s intro — environment reacts first  
2. Personality in 3 lines: funny → unsettling → sincere  
3. Gimmick = emotional metaphor  
4. Phase shift = confession  
5. Defeat ≠ Noise destroyed  

### Emperor McRot (03)
- **Gimmick:** Value Menu Chains — “deals” buff boss if player repeats patterns  
- **Theme:** Comfort food as control  
- **Defeat:** “Tell them… I was delicious…”

### Eternal Buffer (06)
- **Gimmick:** Turn skew / cached attacks  
- **Theme:** Anxiety of waiting  

### Queen Algorithmia (08)
- **Gimmick:** Trend meter — repeated moves get countered  
- **Theme:** Comparison as violence  

### Abbot Endless Feed (14)
- **Gimmick:** Timeline HP — longer fight = stronger phases  
- **Theme:** Doomscrolling as prayer  

### THE CORE FEED (25)
- **Phases:** Recommendation → Engagement Lock → Identity Merge → Null Trend  
- **Lines:** “We picked this battle just for you.” / “Stay. The ladder goes higher if you stay.”  
- **Defeat:** “Okay! New content loading!” — **season reset twist**
